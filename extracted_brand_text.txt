SECTION 02
The Logo 02
The primary logo unites three elements: the illustrative massage emblem
within a golden ring, the botanical sprig, and the Cinzel wordmark. It
is the definitive expression of the brand and should be used wherever
space allows.
THE EMBLEM
The ringed massage illustration
may stand alone as an icon for
avatars, stamps and small formats.
THE WORDMARK
“AURORA” in Cinzel Bold above
“International Spa” in Cinzel
Regular, flanked by dashes.
BACKGROUNDS
Reserve the logo for ivory, espresso
and sage fields. Avoid busy or low-
contrast surfaces.

SECTION 05
Typography 05
Two typefaces carry the whole identity — the engraved elegance of Cinzel for
names and titles, and the literary warmth of Cormorant Garamond for all
that is read at length.
Aa
CINZEL
Serif · Display
PRIMARY TYPEFACE Names, titles & headings
AURORA Regular · 400
AURORA SemiBold · 600
AURORA Bold · 700
Aa
CORMORANT GARAMOND
Old-style Serif · Text
SUPPORTING TYPEFACE Body, quotes & long copy
An unhurried sanctuary of natural
touch & quiet, restorative ritual.
Set body copy in Regular at generous leading; reserve the italic
for quotations and captions, and SemiBold for gentle emphasis.
THE PAIRING IN PRACTICE
The Art of Stillness
A moment composed with intention
Cinzel sets the name and the heading; Cormorant Garamond carries the sentiment
beneath. Together they hold the calm, considered voice of the Aurora identity.

SECTION 06
Elements & Voice 06
THE MOTIF & ORNAMENT
RITUAL · REST ·
RENEW
The dash-and-diamond rule from the wordmark
is our signature divider. Pair it with the
botanical sprig from the emblem to frame
headings and separate content.
PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION
Favour soft, diffused daylight, warm neutral tones, natural
textures and unposed calm. Avoid harsh shadows, cool
casts and clinical settings.
TONE OF VOICE
We are
Warm, calm and reassuring. We speak simply
and sincerely, like a trusted host — “Breathe.
You are cared for here.”
We avoid
Hype, jargon and pressure. No exclamation-
heavy sales talk or clinical language — nothing
that breaks the sense of ease.
